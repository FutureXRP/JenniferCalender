Set up your team under 👥 Team & PTO — each person's weekly schedule builds every new month automatically, and PTO swaps in the covering employee on just those days. Each month is saved separately, so edits to one month never change another. Click inside any day to edit it by hand, select a name and click a color to highlight it (↩ Undo steps back), and use 📱 Text as PDF to send the month from your phone.

Employees & weekly schedule

Add each employee, set their status, and check the days of the week they normally work — the calendar builds itself from this and stays in sync as you make changes here. PRN (as-needed) people have no weekly days: click 📍 Pick days, then click calendar days to add or remove them. Once you edit a month by hand, that month is locked in and won't change on its own ("Rebuild this month" refreshes it if you ever want that).

PTO / time off

Pick who's off, the dates, and an optional cover person — nothing touches the calendar until you press ✓ Confirm. This list shows the month you're viewing; time off from other months stays saved as history and never carries into a new month's schedule. Press ✕ any time to take the time off back off the calendar.

Holidays

On a holiday only the two people you pick work — everyone else is off that day. Nothing changes until you press ✓ Confirm; ✕ removes the holiday and brings back the normal schedule.
