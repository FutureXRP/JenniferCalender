Employees & weekly schedule

Add each employee and check the days of the week they normally work — the calendar builds itself from this and stays in sync as you make changes here. Once you edit a month by hand, that month is locked in and won't change on its own ("Rebuild this month" refreshes it if you ever want that).

PTO / time off

When someone is off, their name comes off those days on the calendar. Pick a cover person to put in their slot (or leave it blank to show them as PTO). Only the affected days change — the rest of the month stays as-is.
